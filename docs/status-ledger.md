| 15 | CPU voltage binning (PVTM/eFuse) | Design only; no patch, branch, or build exists as of 2026-07-27. The 2026-07-25 comparison established that mainline ships the BSP's unbinned worst-die CPU voltage column exactly, but could not say what this die was entitled to because its `cpul_opp_info`/`cpub01_opp_info` cells are blank and PVTM is a runtime measurement. Booting the vendor BSP on this board resolves it: `bin=0`, `leakage=15/13/13`, `pvtm=1525/1782/1778`, `pvtm-volt-sel=5/7/7`, each index reproducing from its cluster's `rockchip,pvtm-voltage-sel` bucket table. Two independent live confirmations that the index is applied rather than merely computed — `policy0` at 1800000 kHz reads `vdd_cpu_lit_s0` at 887500 µV, exactly cluster0's `opp-microvolt-L5` and 62.5 mV below the 950000 the forward port applies at the same OPP; and `supported_hw[1] = BIT(7) = 0x80` gates the turbo steps to 2400 alone, which is what the BSP exposes while 2256/2304/2352 are absent. Entitlement is therefore −37.5 to −87.5 mV on nine of the ten non-trivial CPU OPPs, ≈−20% dynamic power on the big clusters at 1800/2016, and exactly 0 mV at 2400 MHz — so a `performance`-pinned big cluster gains nothing from the whole mechanism. ## Promotion rule

When a row becomes stable enough to be project-owned, move the detail to the
owning project doc and leave only a link here. When a fact can change without a
repo edit, keep the maintenance trigger in [`../status.md`](../status.md)'s
watchlist rather than only recording it here.
